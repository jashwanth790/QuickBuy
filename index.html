Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about QuickBuy and the project architecture

QuickBuy uses an intelligent rule-based scoring algorithm implemented in vanilla JavaScript. It matches your chosen shopping purpose (+35 points), style (+30 points), and product category (+25 points), checks whether the item price comfortably fits within your selected budget (+20 points), and adds a customer rating multiplier. The highest-ranked items are presented along with dynamic explanation badges and budget utilization tracking.

You can click the coupon badge QUICK10 in the Special Offers section to copy it to your clipboard. Then open your cart drawer, paste it into the coupon input field, and click Apply. The cart will automatically deduct 10% from your order subtotal and show real-time feedback.
